### Merry Go Round (MGR) + Freaky Arrows 
https://raidplan.io/plan/qD9Y_g1caq3l5gD- Merry Go Round is the big box arrow placement. The difference is that the cardinal arrows need to be placed on the inner edge of the waymark. Do NOT use static spots for Sleep and Confuse debuffs.
<Details title="Graven Image">

### Graven 1: 
- Ranged far, Melees close (for spread)
- Supports West, DPS East (for stack and confetti puddle drop)
- Conga **HHTTMMRR**, left to right prio. G2 → G1 (H2, H1, T2, T1, etc.) 
### Graven 2:
- **Quadstack Puddles**: Gravitas (confetti) puddles will all be stacked on 1 singular spot (amended from previously double/singular drops in earlier strats). 1st puddle drop North, 2nd puddle drop South. 
- Rock spread: G1 left, G2 right (looking inward at the boss) G1 CW, G2 CCW  
- Gravity players: Go middle
- **Gravitas soak:** Supports start north, ride knockback + soak south. DPS start south, ride knockback + soak north. Confetti players, step back to help soak 
### Graven 3: 
- "Merry Go Round" make large square box with arrows
- Gravitas knockback: Supports NW, DPS SE; confetti debuffs on 1/3 markers 
- Sleep/confuse role tethers: Confuse go out toward cardinal edge; sleep stay close under boss. 
- - MT + R1: North cardinal 
- - OT + R2: West cardinal
- - M1 + H1: South cardinal
- - M2 + H2: East cardinal 
- Spread: Ranged far, melees close.
- Stack: DPS NW (1 marker), Supports SE (3 marker)
